Rules:

1. claim_id must be sequential: C001, C002, C003, ...

2. subject should identify the main entity of the claim whenever possible.
   Examples:

   - "Levofloxacin 750 mg IV" → subject = "Levofloxacin"
   - "Benzylpenicillin allergy" → subject = "Benzylpenicillin"

3. For medication claims:

   - value = numerical dose when applicable
   - unit = dose unit

   - duration = duration
